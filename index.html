Plain-English Unpack

What the Statement Actually Says

Every phrase in the statement is deliberate and has a specific legal meaning. Here is what each line means in plain English, unpacked by key term.

  • “Agreed to this resolution”
    Kevin made a decision to resolve a matter rather than continue to litigate it. Like most civil matters in the United States, resolution does not require admission or finding of fault.
  • “Solely to avoid the significant cost, time, and uncertainty of a lengthy trial”
    The reason given is explicit: cost, time, and uncertainty. This is the standard and most common reason parties settle civil matters. It is not an admission — it is a business decision about litigation economics.
  • “The Consent Judgment”
    A consent judgment is a civil resolution entered with the agreement of both parties. It is not a trial verdict. It is not a criminal conviction. It is a negotiated closure.
  • “Does not include any finding or admission of wrongdoing”
    This is the most important line in the statement. There is no finding — meaning no judge or jury made a determination against Kevin. There is no admission — meaning Kevin did not concede wrongdoing. The resolution documents do not contain either.
  • “On Kevin's part”
    The disclaimer is personal and specific. It addresses Kevin Murdock individually. Any reporting or repetition that implies or asserts personal wrongdoing misrepresents the public record.
